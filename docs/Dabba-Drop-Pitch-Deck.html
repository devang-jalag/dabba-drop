03 / Phased Expansion

Perfect Tiffins First, Then Scale Across Recurring Verticals

Phase 1 (Mo 1-3): Tiffins

  • Target 3-5 local tiffin kitchens and meal-prep operators.
  • Prove zero missed 100m alerts and rapid doorstep drop-off.
  • Add two-way dabba tin recovery counts to stop container loss.

Phase 2 (Mo 4-6): Daily

  • Fresh morning milk and glass-bottle dairy subscriptions.
  • Artisanal bakeries and cold-pressed juice routes.
  • Perishables require immediate 100m porch pickup before spoilage.

Phase 3 (Mo 6-12): Any Parcel

  • Weekly CSA organic vegetable boxes, coffee, and print magazines.
  • Wash-and-fold laundry bags and 20L water jug exchanges.
  • Home pharmacy and medical sample pickups needing 7-day chain of custody.

04 / Monetization & Ad Policy

Clean B2B SaaS Pricing Without Cluttering the Rider Screen

Primary Revenue: B2B SaaS

  • Starter Fleet: $19 / rider / month (up to 1,500 drops).
  • Growth Kitchen: $29 / rider / month or $0.03 per verified drop.
  • Includes iOS/Android driver app, web ledger, and custom SMS/push webhooks.

Ad Policy: Zero Rider Ads

  • Third-party banner ads slow down riders and hurt B2B trust.
  • Optional secondary revenue only on the Order Lookup page.
  • Curated cross-promotions (e.g., local farm boxes, chai, or bakery add-ons).

From Local Pilot Telemetry to Institutional Backing

1. Two-Week Kitchen Pilot

2. Ten Paying Fleets

Convert pilot metrics into a 1-page case study. Close 10 local meal, dairy, and farm-box fleets ($500-$1,500 MRR proof point).

3. Investor Outreach

Pitch food-tech angels, logistics micro-funds, YC/Techstars, and local startup incubators with live route metrics rather than slides alone.
